A LITTLE ABOUT MAELINNE

Everyday favourites.
A little more TLC.

MaeLinne is a small, family-run clothing business born out of necessity, by a mother-and-daughter team with a shared love of comfortable, affordable fashion. Shopping trips were often failures despite loving the outings; fabrics were itchy and tags were scratchy and tops were too short. So we figured we'd just source our own, and that's exactly what we did!

We believe feeling good in what you wear shouldn't come with a hefty price tag. We wanted to bring together soft fabrics, flattering fits and easy-to-wear styles that feel just as lovely as they look.

Our approach is simple: thoughtful choices, good quality and prices that make sense. We're building a collection of wardrobe favourites designed to be worn, loved, shared and enjoyed.

MaeLinne is currently preparing for its first collection, carefully exploring styles and working with suppliers who share our appreciation for quality, comfort and affordability.

We're only just getting started, but we can't wait to share what we've been putting together.
