A LITTLE ABOUT MAELINNE

Everyday favourites.
A little more TLC.

Hey, we're Maelinne and we love finding clothes to get excited about!

Do you adore teen fashion and current trends? Do you like knowing that fabrics are good quality and comfy to wear? Do you love unique one-off pieces but have to keep to a small budget? If so, we're glad you found us.

We love a trend, but we also want to keep wearing our favourites long afterwards. We mix current styles with the pieces you’ll keep coming back to.

We're big fans of the laid back Indie vibe, the fun and friendly Downtown girl and who doesn't love a bit of Y2K energy?! Mix it all together and you've got what Maelinne's Boutique is all about.

And because your budget matters, we keep prices affordable. Affordable clothes you love, with enough left over for going out in them!

Take a look around, find something you love and style it your way. Have fun!
